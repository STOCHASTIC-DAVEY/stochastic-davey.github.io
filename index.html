About

I completed my PhD under Lorenzo Zambotti at the Laboratoire de Probabilités, Statistique et Modélisation (LPSM), and a postdoctoral position under Antoine Gloria at the Laboratoire Jacques-Louis Lions (LJLL), both at Sorbonne Université in Paris, France. I am now a visiting researcher at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) in Sydney, Australia, under the guidance of Anthony Dooley. My research is at the intersection of probability, analysis and partial differential equations (PDEs).
